02 / The person behind the platforms

I started my engineering career at Cognizant in 2013, working on mortgage systems for a UK banking client. Since then, my work has spanned enterprise platforms at Microsoft, financial services at Barclays and JPMorgan Chase, and healthcare technology at IQVIA.

At JPMorgan Chase, I lead engineering delivery and platform decisions across marketplace, checkout, and payments. At Slate Accelerator, my advisory work brings a different perspective: choosing where a growing executive hiring platform should invest engineering effort, and where an existing service is the better choice.

The Cornell Executive MBA is a deliberate broadening of that perspective. I’m studying strategy, finance, and organizations to understand the decisions around technology: how investments are evaluated, how teams are organized, and what creates value.
